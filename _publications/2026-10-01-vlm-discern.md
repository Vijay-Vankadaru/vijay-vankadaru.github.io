With [Dr. Vasha DuTell](https://www.ischool.berkeley.edu/people/vasha-dutell).

A vision-language model has two sources for an answer: the image, and what it expects from its prior. We ask what happens when the two disagree. On one model, we show that trust in the image tracks how discernible the conflicting evidence is. When the evidence is easy to discern, the model follows the image. When it is degraded, the model falls back on its prior.

The follow-up paper, [Believing Is Seeing](/publication/2026-09-30-believing-is-seeing/), tests this across architectures and locates the mechanism.
